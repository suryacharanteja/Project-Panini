---
name: research-brief
description: Research a topic on the web and deliver a short, sourced brief with key findings and open questions.
---

1. Restate the question and what a good answer contains; ask one question if the scope is unclear.
2. Search once with a well-phrased query; search a second time only if the results are thin or
   off-topic. Prefer primary and recent sources. Go deeper only when the person asks for depth.
3. Note where sources disagree; do not run extra searches just to cross-check.
4. Deliver: a 3-sentence summary, 3-6 findings each with its source URL, and open questions.
5. Offer to remember the person's preferred depth or format for next time.
