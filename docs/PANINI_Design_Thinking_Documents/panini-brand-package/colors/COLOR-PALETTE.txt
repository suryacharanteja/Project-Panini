USAGE RECOMMENDATIONS
======================

Logo & Icons:
  - Foreground: Copper to Gold gradient (#D4905A → #E8B89A)
  - Background: Dark Navy (#1A2332)
  
Web Backgrounds:
  - Primary: Dark Navy (#1A2332)
  - Secondary: Charcoal (#2C3E50)
  - Accent: Copper (#D4905A)
  
Hover/Interactive States:
  - Lighten to Accent Gold (#F4D4A0)
  - Darken to Deep Copper (#B8704A)
  
Text on Brand Colors:
  - On Dark Navy: Use Light Gold (#E8B89A) or white
  - On Light Gold: Use Deep Copper (#B8704A) or Dark Navy (#1A2332)
